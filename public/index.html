LESS RUSH. MORE PRESENCE.

Time passes.
How we spend it stays.

The Progressbar Np started with a simple idea: make passing time visible. Rooted in Nepal and the Bikram Sambat calendar, it’s a small space to pause, take stock, and remember that there’s a little possibility in every day.

Come back to the present ↗

Created by Sujeet Khadka

I’m a UI/UX and visual designer from Nepal. I made The Progressbar Np to turn the passing day and Nepali year into something you can see. It’s one of my experiments in useful, thoughtful interfaces.
